implementation are the work of **BitApes Labs**:

- Implementation: <https://github.com/bitapeslabs/mezcal>

Every normative rule on this documentation site was read from that
implementation, at revision `0f3323ffc1c657ad529529f04543b5ba93250fd6`. Where the
upstream README and the upstream code disagree, this documentation follows the

code is referenced and described, not copied into this repository.

Mezcal names, symbols, and artwork remain associated with their respective
creators. Their credited use here does not imply affiliation or endorsement in
either direction.

## What Bitcoin Universe contributes

- The prose, structure, diagrams, and worked examples on this site.
- The test vectors, derived from the rules in the upstream implementation.
- The client-side payload validator and builder.
- Clearly marked Bitcoin Universe indexing and marketplace decisions, such as
  computing circulating supply as total supply minus burnt supply, publishing a
  maximum supply only when the terms prove one, and deriving a marketplace
  purchase instruction byte for byte from a verified listing.

These are published under the repository [LICENSE](LICENSE).
